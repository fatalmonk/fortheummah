### Cancellation, returns, refunds, damage, and missing pieces
- **Cancellation before production/preparation:** Allowed. If cancelled before production/preparation begins, the customer receives a full refund of the deposit. Once production/preparation has begun, this cancellation right no longer applies.
- **Inspection/rejection at delivery:** Customer may inspect the product in front of the delivery courier. If the customer does not accept it after inspection, the order may be rejected/cancelled at delivery. The 50% deposit is refunded minus applicable delivery and return-courier charges.
- **Returns and exchanges after acceptance:** No change-of-mind returns or exchanges after the customer has inspected and accepted the product from the courier. Defective, damaged, or missing-piece claims are handled separately.
- **Claim window:** Damage, manufacturing defects, or missing pieces must be reported within **3 days of delivery**.
- **Damaged/defective item remedy:** For a genuine manufacturing defect or qualifying damage that could not reasonably be identified during courier inspection, the customer may choose either a **free replacement or full refund**.
- **Missing pieces remedy:** If a puzzle is confirmed to have missing pieces, the customer receives a **free complete-puzzle replacement**.
- **Refund processing:** Approved refunds are processed within **7 business days**.
- **Refund method:** Same payment method originally used, where possible.
